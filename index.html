FAQ

Common questions.

What products and services does Sairam Powertech offer?

We provide batteries, inverters, solar power solutions, and CCTV security systems for homes, offices, shops, and commercial spaces. We also offer installation, maintenance, replacement, and technical support..

How do I choose the right battery or inverter for my needs?

The right system depends on your power requirements, the number of appliances you want to run, backup duration, and budget. Our team can assess your requirements and recommend a suitable battery and inverter combination.

Do you provide solar panel installation services?

Yes. We provide end-to-end solar solutions, including system selection, installation, setup, and support. Our team can help you choose a solar system based on your electricity usage and available space.

How can I get a quotation or consultation?

Simply contact us through our phone number, WhatsApp, or contact form. Share your requirements with us, and our team will help you with the suitable solution and quotation.

Do you provide CCTV installation and maintenance?

Yes. We provide CCTV installation solutions for homes, offices, shops, and commercial properties. We can help with camera selection, installation, configuration, and ongoing maintenance.space.
